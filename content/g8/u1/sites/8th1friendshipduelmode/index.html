2-Player Duel Mode Rules:

  • Two players face off in an infinite duel
  • Each round, both players get 1 question
  • Click directly on the Turkish meaning to submit your answer
  • Correct answer = 1 point, you stay in the game
  • Wrong answer = you're eliminated!
  • Game continues until one player is eliminated
  • After both players answer, click "Next Round" to continue
  • No round limit - duel continues until a winner is decided!
  • The last player standing wins the duel!

Note: Enter player names before starting the duel. Answers are submitted immediately when you click an option.
