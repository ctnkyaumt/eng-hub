How to Play:

  • Each player answers 1 question per round (5 rounds total)
  • Click directly on the Turkish meaning to submit your answer
  • If you answer correctly, you get 1 point and stay in the game
  • If you answer incorrectly, you are eliminated from the game
  • After all players answer, click "Next Round" to continue
  • After 5 rounds, if 2 or more players remain, an "Extra Round" button will appear
  • In Extra Round: All players get the SAME word. First to answer correctly WINS!
  • The last player standing (or first correct in extra round) wins the game!

Note: Enter player names before starting the game. Answers are submitted immediately when you click an option.
